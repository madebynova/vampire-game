The Prototype

Is being this vampire actually fun?

Vampire Game is a third-person, single-player vampire action-adventure — for now a small, deliberate prototype built in Godot. It exists to answer one question: does it feel like you became one?

You begin in the coffin room of the Blackthorn Estate as a Human. Change, and the night opens around you: you are faster, hungrier, and you can sense what the living cannot. But the day is coming, and a few minutes of full noon sun is enough to end you.

The systems are small on purpose. They are meant to collide — sunlight against feeding, time of day against what you are — so that every choice costs something.

Mechanics

What the night is made of

Everything below exists in the current prototype. No concept art, no mock-ups.

Human ↔ Vampire

Two bodies. One hunger.

Change, and the world tightens into a red-black tunnel, the body arches, time stutters, and the night opens around you. Going back is quieter: a long exhale, warmth, the cloak turning to ash.

Feeding

Every victim drinks differently.

A sleeper gives a long, gentle Dreamblood. A terrified victim screams, pays more, and leaves a hot, short Fury — and anyone who sees it runs. A good feed leaves a Bloodrush. A fox is a quieter, smaller drink.

Day & Night

A full day lasts 20 real minutes.

The sky turns on a steady clock, and the people of the estate keep to routines. Night belongs to you. Day does not, and the coffin is where you wait it out.

Sunlight

The sun is a clock, and it's running.

About three minutes of full noon sun will kill you. Shadow stops being scenery and becomes a decision — and a fresh Bloodrush buys you a little more patience.

Vampiric Sense

See what the living can't.

Toggle Sense and the night answers: heartbeats sound different calm, asleep or afraid; strangers flicker while people you know burn steady. It shows people, animals, hidden things — even the walls you can scale. It costs blood, unless you're riding a Bloodrush.

Trust & Talk

People who start to tell you things.

Speak with the estate's people as a Human — a groundskeeper, a seamstress, a night watchman — and as they come to trust you they share what they know: where the foxes den, who sleeps where, what they are afraid of.

Blood Memories

Drink a life. Live a moment.

Feeding can open a memory: the world freezes and drains into its colour, and the story is told a few words at a time. Sixteen are hidden among the people and the foxes — some only trust will open, and the deepest waits until you've heard the rest.

The Coffin Loop

Home is a box with a lid.

The coffin is your refuge and your reset. Lie down and it asks when you'll wake: dusk, midnight, dawn — or daylight, to rise as a Human and talk to people in the sun.

Mist & Stone

Only a vampire goes this way.

Dissolve to mist at a window sill, scale the manor wall to its roof, or drop through the broken roof — but only on purpose, from the right place, facing the way you mean to go.

Development Status

A prototype, still being built

Public development build

This is not the finished game — it's an early prototype, shared openly while it's made. The world is a single greybox location, the audio is placeholder, and there are rough edges. Expect things to change, break and be rebuilt. The browser version of the game isn't available yet.

  1. Task 1The core loop works
  2. Task 1.5It interacts: day/night, sun, routines
  3. Task 1.75It feels like something
  4. Task 1.8 currentReliable, purposeful world & traversal
  5. Browser build plannedPlay in your browser from this site

Not in yet — on purpose

  • Wolf / Bat forms
  • Combat
  • Quests
  • Inventory & crafting
  • Skill trees
  • Turning people & lineage
  • A large world
  • Save games
  • Multiplayer
  • A mod manager

Known limits today

  • Greybox environment — no final art or UI
  • Placeholder procedural audio, no music
  • One small location: three people, two foxes
  • Simple NPC pathing
  • Traversal uses authored routes, not free climbing
  • Controllers not yet tried on hardware
  • No built executable or web build yet

Try it today

The prototype runs from the Godot project:

  1. Install Godot 4.8 (standard build).
  2. Clone or download the repository.
  3. In Godot: Import → pick project.godot.
  4. Press F5.
