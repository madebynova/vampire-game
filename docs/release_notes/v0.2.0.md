## Vampire Game 0.2.0 - THE HUNT

The first real gameplay loop. Version 0.1.0 had a vampire with plenty of ways to move, sense and feed, and not much to point them at. 0.2.0 adds **one threat, one attack and one objective**, and makes the things you already had matter in a fight. It is still an early prototype: one small greybox location (Blackthorn Estate), placeholder audio, no music.

### What's new
- **A hunter walks Blackthorn after dark.** He keeps a camp in the pines behind the manor, comes out at dusk and walks a round by lantern. He sees you by a cone (further in lamplight, less in the dark), hears you by how fast you move, and feels you at his back. Once he has noticed you he runs you down - through doorways: **he cannot use a window or climb a roof** - and swings a blade you can see coming. At dawn he withdraws.
- **Rend**, the vampire's one attack (**R**, left mouse button, or **RB / R1**). A plain strike does 20 of his 120; at a run it is a **pounce** (25), falling onto him a **plunge** (30), and against someone who has not seen you an **ambush** that hits three times as hard. It costs a little blood and gives a taste back; hunger weakens it, a Bloodrush strengthens it.
- **Your old powers matter.** Vampiric Sense tells you whether he has noticed you, whether he is hunting you, and when his back is turned. Roofs and windows break a chase. The dark hides you and lamplight gives you away. Turn Human and he is not sure what he saw. A vampire mends its wounds on blood, so being hurt is a debt.
- **Drink him.** Put him down and he lies there, alive. His blood is the richest in the estate, leaves the longest, strongest Bloodrush, and holds a memory of his own. Leave him, and he is back at dusk.
- **One quiet line says what is going on**, under the corner of the screen. It reads the world instead of keeping a quest log, and picks up clues you already had: the wax under the north window, the scratched gate lock, the watchman's log, what Elise sees - and two new things to read at his camp.
- **Readable fights**: a ring on the centre dot that fills while Rend swings, hit marks, a red arc pointing at whoever struck you, a bar and a "!" over his head, floating damage, a **WOUNDED** line, the camera sliding over your shoulder while he hunts you, and a handful of new sounds.

### Controls
Rend is **R**, the **left mouse button**, or **RB** (Xbox) / **R1** (PlayStation). Everything else is as before; press **H** for the controls screen.

### Your settings
Still stored in `%APPDATA%\VampireGame`, outside the game folder.

### Known limitations
- This build was tested by automated suites (about 1,080 checks, including inside the exported build) and by a script that plays the whole hunt with real key events. It has **not been played by hand**, and **controllers have still not been tried on physical hardware**. The new sounds have not been heard by the developer; balance (his 120 health and 26 damage, the length of his tell) is from a bot, so it is the number most worth your feedback.
- One hunter. After you drink him the estate is as quiet as it was in 0.1.0.
- No save games, no music, greybox visuals, one small location, not code-signed (see 0.1.0's notes about SmartScreen).
